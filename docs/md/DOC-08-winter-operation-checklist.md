# DOC-08 Winter Operation Checklist

Document ID: DOC-08 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Fleet Maintenance | Applies to: all vehicles, from 1 November to 31 March or whenever temperatures fall below 0 C

> Training document written for a portfolio project. Figures are illustrative and not official guidance.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Cold weather affects starting, fuel, batteries, brakes and tires. This checklist adds winter steps to the normal pre-trip inspection (DOC-01).

## 2. Cold starts and block heaters

1. When the temperature is below -10 C, plug in the engine block heater at least 2 hours before the planned start.
2. Do not crank the engine for more than 15 seconds at a time. Wait 2 minutes between attempts.
3. Let the engine idle and warm up before driving off. Do not rev a cold engine.

## 3. Fuel

1. Use winter-blend diesel, or add anti-gel additive, when temperatures are below -10 C.
2. Drain the fuel-water separator every day in winter.
3. Keep fuel tanks at least half full to limit condensation.

## 4. Engine oil

Use the approved 5W-40 synthetic when regular temperatures are below -15 C. Grades and intervals are in DOC-03.

## 5. Batteries

1. Have batteries load-tested before winter. Cold reduces battery power sharply.
2. If a vehicle will not start, follow DOC-06 for the jump-start steps. Never jump-start a frozen battery.

## 6. Air brakes

1. Drain air tanks every day. Water in the system can freeze and block brake valves.
2. Check the air dryer and replace the cartridge at the scheduled interval.
3. Do not use the parking brake on a wet, freezing vehicle left standing for long periods if it can be avoided. Use wheel chocks.

## 7. Tires

Tire pressure falls about 1 psi for every 5 C drop in temperature, so check pressures at least once a week and at every pre-trip inspection (see DOC-04).

## 8. Lights, glass and snow

1. Clean all lights, mirrors and glass before moving.
2. Use washer fluid rated to -40 C.
3. Clear snow and ice from the roof, hood and trailer top before driving off.

## 9. Winter emergency kit

Each vehicle carries a warm blanket, flashlight with spare batteries, reflective triangles, tire chains where required, a shovel, and a high-visibility vest.
