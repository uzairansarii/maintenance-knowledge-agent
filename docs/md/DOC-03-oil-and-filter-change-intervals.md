# DOC-03 Oil and Filter Change Intervals

Document ID: DOC-03 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Fleet Maintenance | Applies to: all company vehicles

> Training document written for a portfolio project. Figures are illustrative and not official guidance.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document sets engine oil and filter change intervals, oil grades, the change procedure and how to dispose of used oil.

## 2. Change intervals

1. Tractor units: every 25,000 km or every 12 months, whichever comes first.
2. Light vehicles (vans and pickups): every 10,000 km or every 6 months, whichever comes first.
3. Yard trucks and shunt tractors: every 500 engine hours or every 12 months, whichever comes first.
4. Severe service (long idling, short trips, dusty sites): shorten the interval by 30 percent.
5. The oil filter is always changed with the oil.

## 3. Oil grades

1. Standard grade for the fleet is 15W-40 diesel engine oil.
2. When regular temperatures are below -15 C, use the approved 5W-40 synthetic instead (see DOC-08).
3. Never mix oil grades in one engine. Do not top up with a different grade unless it is an emergency, and then log it.

## 4. Change procedure

1. Run the engine briefly to warm the oil, then shut it off.
2. Place the drain pan under the sump and remove the drain plug. Let the oil drain fully.
3. Remove the old oil filter. Check that the old gasket came off with it.
4. Wipe the filter mount, lightly oil the new filter gasket and fit the new filter as the filter maker instructs.
5. Refit the drain plug with a new washer. Tighten to the engine maker's specification.
6. Fill with the correct amount of new oil, then run the engine and check for leaks.
7. Shut off, wait a few minutes, and recheck the dipstick level. Top up if needed.

## 5. Used oil and filter disposal

1. Put used oil in the labelled used-oil drum. Never pour it down a drain or onto the ground.
2. Drain used filters for at least 12 hours, then put them in the used-filter bin.
3. Clean up spills at once with absorbent and place the used absorbent in the oily-waste bin.

## 6. Records

Update the service sticker in the cab door frame with the date and odometer or hour reading. Record the service in the maintenance log.
