# DOC-07 Defect Reporting Procedure

Document ID: DOC-07 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Fleet Maintenance | Applies to: all drivers, mechanics and supervisors

> Training document written for a portfolio project. Figures are illustrative and not official guidance.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document explains how any defect on a company vehicle is reported, categorised, repaired and closed.

## 2. Who must report

Everyone. If you see a defect, you report it, even if you think someone else already did. Reporting a defect never gets you in trouble. Not reporting one can.

## 3. Defect categories and repair times

1. Category 1, critical: the vehicle is unsafe to move. Examples: brake defects, a failed air leak-down test, steering defects, tires with exposed cord or bulges, loose or missing wheel nuts, and a running or spraying hydraulic leak. Do not move the vehicle. Repair before any further use.
2. Category 2, major: the vehicle can run but needs repair soon. Examples: a dripping hydraulic leak, an inoperative headlight, a cracked windshield outside the driver's view. Repair within 24 hours.
3. Category 3, minor: no safety impact. Examples: a worn seat cover, a broken cab light. Repair within 7 days.
4. If you are not sure which category applies, treat it as the higher one and ask your supervisor.

## 4. How to report

1. Complete the defect report form in the fleet app, or the paper form if the app is not available.
2. Give the vehicle ID, odometer reading, location, date and time, a clear description, and a photo if you can take one safely.
3. Tell your supervisor in person or by radio for any Category 1 defect. Do not rely on the form alone.

## 5. Tagging

Put a red tag on the steering wheel or key for every Category 1 defect. Only a supervisor or lead mechanic may remove a red tag.

## 6. Repair, sign-off and return to service

1. The mechanic records the repair and parts used on the defect form.
2. A supervisor signs off before a vehicle with a Category 1 defect returns to service.
3. The driver who reported the defect is told when it is closed.

## 7. Records

Defect forms are kept for 24 months in the vehicle file.
