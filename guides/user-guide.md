# Garage Maintenance Assistant: quick guide for staff

## What it is
A chat assistant that answers maintenance questions from the company's maintenance documents. It tells you which document and section the answer came from.

## How to use it
1. Open the assistant (Teams or the link your supervisor gives you).
2. Type your question in normal words, for example:
   - "What is the brake inspection interval for a tractor unit?"
   - "I found a hydraulic leak. What do I do?"
   - "How do I jump-start a truck safely?"
3. Read the numbered steps and check the **Source** line at the end.
4. If the answer matters for safety, do what it says and **confirm with your supervisor** before you start.

## Tips for better answers
- Say which vehicle or system you mean ("tractor unit brakes", not just "brakes").
- Ask one question at a time.
- If it asks you a question back, answer it. It is trying to find the right document.

## When it says "I don't have that information"
The documents do not cover your question. The assistant does not guess. The question is logged so the maintenance team can review it. For anything urgent or safety-related, ask your supervisor or check the manufacturer's manual.

## What it will not do
- It does not replace your supervisor, the vehicle manual or your training.
- It does not diagnose faults it has no document for.
- It does not know about repairs or events on a specific vehicle. Use the defect report form for that.

## If an answer looks wrong
Tell your supervisor and report it to the maintenance team. Do not act on an answer that seems unsafe.
